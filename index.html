Itinerary and time schedule, V17

Your time card

Enter how many minutes after the first car you are due (your start order for that leg). Due times and the light at each stage start update for your car. Sunset 17:03; dark from about 17:33.

How to read it

  • The target time on a TC row is counted from the previous TC. On the row after a special stage it is counted from the stage start: it includes the stage itself, the stop control and the road section.
  • Example, Friday: SS2 starts 14:28. TC2A is due 15:03, so 35 min covers the 13.95 km stage, the stop and 4.65 km of road. Then 15 min for 6.50 km to TC3, then 3 min to the SS3 start.
  • From each TC before a stage you have 3 min to the start line (TC to start distance is in the road section column of the SS row).

Things that look unusual

  • TC2A, Collobrières town hall (Friday). An extra time control in the village centre between SS2 and SS3. No service, no regroup: check in and carry on. The documents do not state why it is there.
  • TC3 / TC8 / TC10 "RD41, St Guillaume". This is simply the place name of the time control before the Babaou stage: on the D41, 50 m before the start line. Same point all three times.
  • SS10. The timing gives 0.30 km from TC10 to the start, but 0.05 km for SS3 and SS8 at the same place. Probably a typing error: check the road book.
  • The real time card (carnet de bord) is not published online. It is handed to the crew. This table is built from the official time schedule V17, which the time card follows; TC2A is listed there as a time control, so expect it on the card.
Reconnaissance

Recce plan

Rules (regulations art. 6.2): one session only, weekend or weekday. Maximum 2 passes per stage. No going back or looping on a stage. 2 people on board, standard car with tripmaster, map light and 2 extra lamps. Respect speed limits: the tracker's "Mode Reco" records speed in declared zones.
